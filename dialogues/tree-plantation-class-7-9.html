Tree Plantation Dialogue for Class 7-9

×

Rafi: Hi, Sohan. What are you doing? Sohan: I am planting a tree in our school yard. Rafi: Why are you planting trees? Sohan: Trees are very useful to us. Rafi: How do trees help us? Sohan: They give us oxygen and keep the air clean. Rafi: Do trees give us anything else? Sohan: Yes. They give us fruits, wood, flowers, and shade. Rafi: Do trees help the environment? Sohan: Yes. They keep the weather cool and help bring rain. Rafi: What happens if we cut down too many trees? Sohan: It causes many problems like floods, heat, and soil loss. Rafi: Then we should plant more trees. Sohan: Yes. Everyone should plant trees. Rafi: When is the best time to plant trees? Sohan: We can plant trees in the rainy season. Rafi: We should also take care of them. Sohan: Of course. A young tree needs water and care. Rafi: Let's plant some more trees together. Sohan: Great idea. Trees are our best friends.
